# Planning Campaigns

Preparation is the key to success, and this is especially true with programmatic SEO. When you launch a pSEO campaign, you provide a bunch of different starting points and let pSEO systems combine and remix them to produce content in bulk.

It’s essential that these starting points are good and that our path to generating content is sound. What we get out is only as good as what we put in.
