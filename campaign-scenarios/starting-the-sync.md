# Starting the Sync

You're ready to sync once your data is in your content store and looking nice.

{% hint style="info" %}
&#x20;While Findable is in early access, the Findable team will manually enable your sync. Once you’re ready to go, reach out, and we’ll turn everything on.
{% endhint %}

During the sync process, Findable will create combination pages based on the data you put into your table. If you're a Concierge customer, these collections will be pre-generated for you.
