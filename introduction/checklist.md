# Checklist

When setting up your Findable campaign, you should follow a few steps:

* [ ] Identify your head term and plan your campaign
* [ ] Find the data you need to create your content
* [ ] Add your data to Findable collections
* [ ] Generate your combo collections
* [ ] Configure a campaign
* [ ] Sync and design your pages
* [ ] Monitor your performance and update content where needed

If you get stuck along the way, don’t hesitate to reach out to the Findable Wizards. Drop a message in our [community Slack ](https://join.slack.com/t/findablecommunity/shared_invite/zt-28e6vf7z7-QaB_EmJrleK_6xSPxljz_Q)and we’ll get back to you as soon as possible!
